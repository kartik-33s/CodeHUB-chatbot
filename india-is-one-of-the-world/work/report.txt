India in the World, A Global Progress Dashboard
Problem Statement
India is one of the world's largest and fastest-growing economies. However, a country's progress cannot be measured by economic growth alone.
Every year, leading international organizations such as the United Nations, World Bank, IMF, WHO, UNESCO, World Economic Forum, Transparency International, Reporters Without Borders, Oxford Insights, and many others publish global rankings and indices that evaluate countries across areas like economy, education, healthcare, governance, innovation, environment, technology, equality, and quality of life.
These datasets are scattered across hundreds of reports, making it difficult for citizens, students, researchers, journalists, policymakers, and businesses to understand where India stands on the global stage.
Your challenge is to build a modern, interactive platform that consolidates these global indicators into a single destination. The platform should help users explore India's rankings, compare them with other countries, analyze historical trends, understand the reasons behind each ranking, and generate meaningful insights using AI.
The goal is not simply to display rankings, but to transform complex global data into an accessible, transparent, and engaging experience that helps users answer one fundamental question:
How is India performing compared to the rest of the world, and where should it improve next?
________________


Objective
Build a web application that aggregates trusted global datasets and enables users to:
* View India's rankings across multiple international indices.
* Compare India with any country.
* Explore historical trends and ranking changes.
* Visualize data using interactive charts, maps, and dashboards.
* Generate AI-powered summaries and insights.
* Access the original source behind every metric.
* Understand India's strengths and areas for improvement through data-driven storytelling.

Core Features
Participants should build a platform that includes:
* Global dashboard for India
* Country comparison dashboard
* Interactive world map
* Historical trend analysis
* Search and filter across all indicators
* Category-wise ranking explorer
* Source attribution for every dataset
* Responsive user interface
* AI-powered insights and summaries

Bonus Features
Teams are encouraged to innovate by including features such as:
* AI chatbot for answering questions about India's rankings.
* Natural language search, such as "Show India's innovation ranking."
* AI-generated annual report cards.

* News integration to explain ranking changes.
